How the implied value is priced

There is no fixed multiple and no list of favourite names. The price comes from the snapshot itself:

  1. Comparable multiple. For each category, the median market-cap / TVL ratio of that category’s live-token protocols (at least 3 of them, otherwise the global median of 0.21). Clamped to 0.05–1.50.
  2. Base. TVL × multiple.
  3. Growth. 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%, times a 0.5 weight: factor = 1 + growth × 0.5.
  4. Maturity. 0.6 + min(age, 3 years) / 3 years × 0.4. A protocol that has run tokenless for years has earned more of its value; one listed last month starts at 60%.
  5. Implied value = base × growth factor × maturity, rounded to the dollar, at least $1.

Pre-token and live-token protocols are never mixed: the board ranks only protocols with no token, no market cap and no Gecko listing. CEXs and versioned clones are excluded. The watchlist — all 40 protocols at or above the $50M floor with no token — is the floor of the board, never its ceiling: the board ranks 289 protocols.
